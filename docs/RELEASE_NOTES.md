# Release notes

## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-02

Initial implementation of Custiva:

- Authentication, recovery, onboarding and multi-tenant workspace.
- Ingredients with yield, effective cost, price history and inventory balance.
- Suppliers, purchases with item allocation, weighted cost and stock receipt.
- Versioned recipes, conversions, costing and circular-dependency protection.
- Packaging, operational expenses and sales channels.
- Pricing simulator with mathematically correct divisor, snapshots and direct-sale channel.
- Inventory ledger, adjustments and FEFO production consumption.
- Dashboard, profitability reports and CSV, Excel and PDF exports.
- RBAC, RLS, private attachments, audit log and documentation.
